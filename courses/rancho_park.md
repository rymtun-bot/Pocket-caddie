## Local rules (scorecard back, golf.lacity.gov, golfer's screenshot, 2026-11-01; partly cut off)
1. OB: perimeter fences, white stakes, white posts/curbs, service yards; #4 outside edge of the road; #9 course side of
   the driving range posts; #18 (a) fence and white lines behind the green, (b) curb on the right of the fairway,
   (c) where there's no curb, the course side of the range posts, (d) left of the low fence left of the fairway.
2. Immovable obstructions include drainage swales, paved roads, staked trees, protective fences (both sides of #1, #4 tee
   area and green, right of #14), wood pilings behind #16 green, range support posts. Dropping zones on #1 for the fences.
3. Drainage ditch on the left of #17 (red stakes) is a penalty area.
5. Ball on the cart path or curb left of the 13th green: optional free drop in the zone behind the 14th tee.

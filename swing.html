1 · Pick a swing video

Record opens the camera (normal speed). For Slo-mo, film with the Camera app's Slo-mo, then choose it from Photos.

The video stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded; only the picture you share in step 4 leaves the phone.

2 · Find each moment

0.000 s

Draw lines: tap two points, for example along the shaft at address, or a line up the stick on the ground. Lines are copied onto the frames you save.

3 · Save the four key frames

Swing check

Save the address and impact frames first. Then tap the points it asks for on each frame.

The checks measure what you tap, so tap carefully on the frame. The limits used (for example, how far the head may move) are rough coaching guides, not published standards; send the result to Claude for a full read.

4 · Send to Claude

Makes one picture with all four frames and your numbers. Share it to the Claude app, or save it to Photos and attach it there.

Tempo trainer (beeps)

Three beeps: low = start the takeaway, middle = top, high = impact. Swing so the club reaches each spot on its beep. A soft tick comes first so you can get set.

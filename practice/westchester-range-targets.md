From the golfer's Google Maps top-down screenshot (2026-11-01). Scale taken from the parked cars next to the range
(about 0.28 yd per screen pixel), so every number is rough: about plus or minus 10% (10-15 yd at 150). Laser one target
from a mat and send the reading; all numbers get rescaled from it.

Mats: the half to the golfer's **left** of the divider when facing the targets (the east half). Mat #1 = the farthest
mat on the left (east end). Direction = how far the target is from straight down the range, as the golfer faces it.

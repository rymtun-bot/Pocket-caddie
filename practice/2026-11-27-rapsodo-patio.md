## Golfer's feels that worked (best compression with the 50°)
1. Weight on the balls of the feet.
2. Standing closer to the ball; lie more neutral (sole flatter).
3. Weight better balanced right and left at address.
4. Takeaway led by the left hand (~90%), then the right hand lets the club hinge on plane.
5. Handle (grip end) pointing at the target line helps the full hinge.
6. Weight shift to the front foot in the downswing.
7. Right forearm controlling the downswing and closing the face.

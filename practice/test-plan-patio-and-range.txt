SHORT GAME (added 11/23; setup that stopped the shanks on 11/22)
Setup for pitches: ball in the middle, hands at the inner left thigh,
60-65% weight left over the arches, head over or just ahead of the ball.
Chips: see the CHIP section in setup-checklist-by-club.txt (50 deg main club).

Clubs and lengths (carries to confirm on the redo):
  54 hip high ~30 | 54 lead arm parallel ~38-40 | 9i hip high ~52
  Full: 58 ~59, 54 ~67, 50 ~74 (11/14), PW ~98-104
  50 and 58: choose for height and roll, not for distance.

Positions
- Hip high: half hinge (shaft about 45 deg up), shaft in line
  with the toe line, toe up, clubhead not tucked behind the hands.
- Lead arm parallel: full hinge ("L"), grip end points at the
  target line.
- Grip end points at or along the target line at every stage,
  never at the feet. Finish at least as long as the backswing.

Speed: chest and hips turn through together; let the club fall;
keep the 1.05/0.35 count. No added hand or arm speed. Want more
distance: use the longer swing, don't swing harder.

Drills
- Headcovers under the armpits: both for hip high and lead arm
  parallel; left armpit only for full swings (right elbow must
  leave the body at the top).
- Ball between the forearms (soccer ball size or smaller, light
  squeeze): hip high and lead arm parallel; full swing only as
  practice swings or 3/4 swings.
- Use each drill as: 3 drill swings, then 1 real ball with the
  same feel, x5 rounds per length.
- Stop and check: swing to the position, hold 1 s, check hinge
  and shaft, then hit. 5 with the stop, 5 without.
- Two-ball check: tee or headcover 1 in outside the ball; hitting
  it = hands moved out toward the ball (shank pattern).
- Face marks: marker or foot powder at the start of the session.
- Launch check (same setup and speed): 50 ~31 deg, 54 ~36 deg,
  58 ~40 deg; club speed within ~2 mph across the three.
- Film face-on once: does the lead arm really reach parallel?

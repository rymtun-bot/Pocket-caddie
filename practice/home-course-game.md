# Home course game: play Westchester or Rancho Park on the patio

Every full shot goes into the net with the Rapsodo and the full pre-shot routine. Chips go to the towel on the patio
mat. Putts are played later, inside on the rug, from the list you write down outside. Score each hole like a real round.

## Before you start
- Rapsodo set up as usual (alignment stick, 7 ft back, ball on the tape mark, net 8 ft).
- Towel (about 3 ft square) on the mat as the chipping target.
- Scorecard, pen, and the club list below. Update the club list as your numbers change.

| Club | Carry used in the game (yd) |
|---|---|
| PW ½ / ¾ / full | measure (PW full about 97 for now) |
| 9-iron | 100 |
| 8-iron | 111 |
| 7-iron | 124 |
| 5U 1 in down | measure |
| 5U | 147 |
| 4U | 154 |
| 5W | 160 |
| 3U / 3W / driver | measure |
Numbers are the golfer's patio carries so far (PW from the Rapsodo; 9-iron to 5W mostly from the PRGR; 7-iron is between
the two monitors' 120 and 128) and are provisional.

## Reading each full shot (from the Rapsodo)
| Result | Start direction | Distance | Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Good** | within 3° of the line | carry within 10% of the target | Approach: on the green. Tee/layup: in the fairway. |
| **Near miss** | 3–6° | carry 10–20% off | Approach: just off the green (chip). Tee/layup: light rough, play on normally. |
| **Miss** | more than 6° | carry more than 20% off | Approach: in trouble (chip + 1 extra stroke). Tee/layup: blocked out, add 1 stroke and play again from the same distance. |
A shot is "close" if it starts within 1.5° and carries within 3% of the target: on the green with a birdie putt.

## Playing a hole
1. **Tee shot.** Par 3: the club whose carry is nearest the hole yardage. Par 4/5: your tee club.
   For tee shots, use the Rapsodo **total** (carry + roll) as the distance gained.
2. **Next shots.** Remaining = yardage minus distance gained. Pick the club nearest the remaining yardage
   (approaches use **carry**). If the remaining yardage is longer than your longest club, hit the longest club you
   trust and play on. With 41–100 yd left: a wedge (PW ½, ¾ or full, whichever carries nearest).
   With 40 yd or less: a pitch, played as a chip to the towel (step 3).
3. **Around the green.** Chip one ball at the towel and write the letter:
   T = on the towel · N = within about one towel-width · M = farther away.
4. **On the green.** Write the putt you get (all putts are played later on the rug).
   Green hit (Good approach): the first putt is a lag your rug can't simulate; count it as 1 stroke, then putt from **3 ft**.
   Close approach: putt from **6 ft** for birdie; a miss = tap-in, +1 stroke.
   After a chip: T = putt from **3 ft**, N = **5 ft**, M = **6 ft**; a miss = tap-in, +1 stroke.
5. **Later, inside:** putt each listed putt once, in order, and finish each hole's score.

## Example: Westchester #1, par 3, 108 yd (Blue)
Tee shot 8-iron (111). Rapsodo: carry 104, 2° right = Good (within 3°, 104 is within 10% of 108) → green.
Lag counted (1 stroke), 3 ft putt on the rug later: made → 3 strokes, par. Missed → 4, bogey.
If it had started 5° right: Near miss → chip to the towel, landed N → 5 ft putt later. Made = par, missed = bogey.

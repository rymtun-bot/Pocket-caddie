DRIVER (gripped down 2 in)
Driver: aim clubface at target first
Driver: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
Driver: stance wider than shoulders
Driver: arms hang straight down from shoulders
Driver: ball off the left heel
Driver: head behind the ball, eyes on the back of the ball
Driver: right shoulder lower, spine tilted away from target
Driver: weight 40% front at setup
Driver: weight 65-75% front at impact, head still behind ball
Driver: full hip turn back (about 45 deg), full turn through
Driver: finish on left heel, right toe, chest to target

3-WOOD
3W: aim clubface at target first
3W: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
3W: stance a little wider than shoulders
3W: arms hang straight down from shoulders
3W: ball 2 ball-widths forward of middle
3W: head slightly behind the ball
3W: right shoulder a little lower
3W: weight 50% front at setup (45% off a tee)
3W: weight 70-80% front at impact, sweep it
3W: full hip turn back, full turn through

HYBRIDS (3U, 4U, 5U)
Hybrid: aim clubface at target first
Hybrid: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
Hybrid: stance shoulder width
Hybrid: arms hang straight down from shoulders
Hybrid: ball 1 ball-width forward of middle
Hybrid: sole flat, stand a little more upright
Hybrid: eyes over or just behind the ball
Hybrid: right shoulder slightly lower
Hybrid: weight 50% front at setup
Hybrid: weight 70-80% front at impact, brush the grass
Hybrid: full hip turn back (about 45 deg)
Hybrid: press left, then turn

IRONS 7i-8i
7i-8i: aim clubface at target first
7i-8i: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
7i-8i: stance shoulder width
7i-8i: arms hang straight down from shoulders
7i-8i: ball just forward of middle
7i-8i: eyes over the ball
7i-8i: shoulders almost level, right slightly lower
7i-8i: weight 50-55% front at setup
7i-8i: weight 75-85% front at impact, hands ahead
7i-8i: hip turn back about 40-45 deg
7i-8i: towel 3-4 in behind ball untouched

IRONS 9i-PW (full)
9i-PW: aim clubface at target first
9i-PW: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
9i-PW: stance shoulder width, slightly narrower
9i-PW: arms hang straight down from shoulders
9i-PW: ball in the middle
9i-PW: eyes over the ball
9i-PW: shoulders almost level
9i-PW: weight 55% front at setup
9i-PW: weight 75-85% front at impact, hands ahead
9i-PW: hip turn back about 40 deg
9i-PW: towel 2-3 in behind ball untouched

WEDGES 50/54/58 (full)
Wedge full: aim clubface at target first
Wedge full: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
Wedge full: stance a bit narrower than shoulders
Wedge full: arms hang straight down from shoulders
Wedge full: ball in the middle
Wedge full: eyes over the ball
Wedge full: shoulders level
Wedge full: weight 55-60% front at setup
Wedge full: weight 75-85% front at impact
Wedge full: hip turn back about 35-40 deg
Wedge full: finish facing target, hold 2 sec

PITCH (hip high / arms parallel)
Pitch: aim clubface at target first
Pitch: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
Pitch: stance narrower than shoulders
Pitch: arms hang straight down from shoulders
Pitch: ball middle or a touch back
Pitch: eyes over the ball
Pitch: shoulders level
Pitch: weight 55-60% front at setup
Pitch: weight 70-80% front at impact
Pitch: small hip turn (about 20-30 deg), chest turns through
Pitch: finish length = backswing length

CHIP
Chip: aim clubface at target first
Chip: feet, hips, shoulders parallel to target line
Chip: stance feet close together
Chip: arms hang straight down from shoulders
Chip: ball back of middle (off right big toe)
Chip: head over or just ahead of the ball
Chip: shoulders level
Chip: weight 60-70% front at setup, stays there
Chip: weight 70-80% front at impact
Chip: almost no hip turn, chest and arms turn together
Chip: hands ahead, no flick

EVERY FULL SWING
All: balls of the feet
All: press left, then turn
All: square the face
All: finish 90-100% on front foot, right heel up

# Short-game plan: pitching and chipping (started 2026-11-27, no end date)

Golfer's goal (2026-11-27): get better at pitching and chipping; not tied to the Dec 1 round.
Setups: practice/setup-checklist-by-club.txt (CHIP) and practice/test-plan-patio-and-range.txt (SHORT GAME: half / full hinge).
Pitch swings: 54° half hinge (38 carry), 54° full hinge (56 carry); tempo 1.05/0.35.
Diagrams: [face-on and from behind](hand-positions-backswing.png), [hand path from above](hand-path-top-view.png).

## Each session (45-60 min, 3-4 times a week)

| Part | Min | What |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | 10 | Towel 6 in behind the ball + tape line in front of it. 50° and 54° half-hinge swings; brush the mat after the line. |
| Chipping | 15 | Landing ladder (towels at 5, 10, 15 ft) with the 50°, then the 54°. Mix clubs and spots: no two shots in a row the same. |
| Pitching | 15 | 54° half hinge and full hinge, 10 each. Rapsodo on every few sessions. |
| Putting | 10 | Gate drill + edge drill on the rug. |

## Week by week (then repeat)
1. Contact and setup: clean strikes, ball first.
2. Distance control: one club, change length / landing spot.
3. Variety: 50°, 54°, 58°; high and low; different lies where possible.
4. Pressure: score everything; repeat any test you fail.

## Scored tests (tick them in Pocket Caddie: Practice drills → Test)
- Chip score: landing ladder, 15 balls (5 per spot). Goal: 10+ land on the towel.
- Pitch score (Rapsodo): 10 half hinge + 10 full hinge. Goal per set: carry spread 6 yd or less, start within ±3°, smash 1.20+.
- Contact score: 10 half-hinge balls with the towel behind. Goal: towel untouched 9 of 10.
- Full-swing hinge test (7i, Rapsodo, 1.05/0.35): 10 normal, then 10 with the full "L" set by lead arm parallel and kept to the top.
  Compare club speed, smash, start. Watch for mat-first contact (early release) and right starts (open face).

Once a week if possible: chips and pitches from real grass or a bunker (a practice green / short-game area).

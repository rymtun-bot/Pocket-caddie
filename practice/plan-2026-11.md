# Two-week patio plan, 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-13

Goal: Clubs tab measured PW through driver on the Rapsodo, one swing fix under way, and a pre-shot routine that runs
on autopilot before the first round in January 2027.

## Every session (about 30–40 minutes)
1. Warm up: easy turns, then 10 hold-off half swings with the PW (hands lead, face to the net, hold the finish).
2. Video swings, if scheduled (iPad, Slo-mo, one clip per swing, in Rapsodo shot order).
3. Club A, 10 shots; club B, 10 shots. Alignment sticks down, Rapsodo 7 ft back on the stick, ball on the tape mark,
   net 8 ft. Wait for the ready signal. Note any missed read and how that shot felt.
4. Course mode, 5 balls: sticks away, full pre-shot routine, a different club every ball.
5. Speed sets on Mon/Wed/Fri: 3 × 5 light swings each side, at the end.
6. Send: Rapsodo shot-list screenshot per club, and video frames or clips with the view named.

## Pre-shot routine (same every shot, about 20–30 seconds)
1. Pocket Caddie (on course): plays-like yardage and club. Aim at the center of the green.
2. Stand behind the ball. Pick the target, then a spot 2–3 ft in front of the ball on that line.
3. One rehearsal swing with the feel you're working on (hold-off: hands lead through impact).
4. Walk in: clubface square to the spot first, then feet, hips and shoulders parallel to that line.
   Hip-hinge setup, ball on its usual position for that club.
5. One look at the target, one look at the ball, swing within about 5 seconds.
6. After the shot: on the course, measure it in Pocket Caddie; on the patio, note start direction.
During a round nothing may be laid down to help alignment (Rules of Golf 10.2b), which is why the spot matters.

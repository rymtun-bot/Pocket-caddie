# Two-week practice plan, 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-13

Goal: Clubs tab measured PW through driver, real carries checked at the range, one swing fix under way, a pre-shot
routine on autopilot, and a short game ready for Westchester or Rancho Park by the first round in January 2027.

## Warm-up (before every session; dynamic movement, no long static holds)
Static stretching held for long periods right before swinging lowered clubhead speed in small studies; dynamic warm-ups
did better. Save long static stretches for after practice or another time of day.
- Patio (about 5 min): 10 arm circles each way; 10 torso turns with a club across the shoulders; 10 hip hinges with the
  club on your back; 10 leg swings each leg; 5 lunges with a turn each side; then 10 hold-off half swings with the PW.
- Range or course (about 10 min): the patio routine, then 10 swings building from half to full speed with a mid iron,
  5 short chips and 5 putts of 3–6 ft before the first tee.
- Stop if anything hurts. Check with a doctor first if you have any joint, back or other concerns.

## Every patio session (about 30–40 min)
1. Warm-up (above).
2. Video swings, if scheduled (iPad, Slo-mo, one clip per swing, in Rapsodo shot order).
3. Club A, 10 shots; club B, 10 shots. Alignment sticks down, Rapsodo 7 ft back on the stick, ball on the tape mark,
   net 8 ft. Wait for the ready signal. Note any missed read and how that shot felt.
4. Course mode, 5 balls: sticks away, full pre-shot routine, a different club every ball.
5. Speed sets on Mon/Wed/Fri: 3 × 5 light swings each side, at the end.
6. Send: Rapsodo shot-list screenshot per club, and video frames or clips with the view named.

## Daily putting (10 min, any day including Thursday; 6 ft rug with the gate trainer)
- Gate drill at 3 ft: 10 putts through the gate. Then 5 ft: 10 putts. Count how many in a row roll cleanly through.
- Finish with 5 putts using the full routine (read, one practice stroke, look at the hole, putt).
- Log the best streak at each distance. Long lag putting happens on range days (a rug is too short).

## Range day (one weekend day, about 90 min; Westchester has a range with mats and a pay-per-use short-game area)
1. Warm-up (range version).
2. Putting green, 30 min: lag ladder from 20, 30 and 40 ft (goal: inside a 3 ft circle, never short); then a circle of
   3–6 ft putts.
3. Short-game area, 30 min: bump-and-run with 8- or 9-iron from 5–20 yd; pitches of 20–40 yd with the sand or gap
   wedge; bunker shots if there is a bunker (goal: out in one).
4. Range mat, 30 min: laser the flags from your stall (note stall number); 10 PW and 10 7-iron at the nearest flags
   with the Rapsodo, noting where each lands vs the flag; Pocket Caddie screenshot for wind and temperature.
   Then a simulated course: play holes in order with the full routine, one ball per shot.

## Pre-shot routine (same every shot, about 20–30 seconds)
1. Pocket Caddie (on course): plays-like yardage and club. Aim at the center of the green.
2. Stand behind the ball. Pick the target, then a spot 2–3 ft in front of the ball on that line.
3. One rehearsal swing with the feel you're working on (hold-off: hands lead through impact).
4. Walk in: clubface square to the spot first, then feet, hips and shoulders parallel to that line.
   Hip-hinge setup, ball on its usual position for that club.
5. One look at the target, one look at the ball, swing within about 5 seconds.
6. After the shot: on the course, measure it in Pocket Caddie; on the patio, note start direction.
During a round nothing may be laid down to help alignment (Rules of Golf 10.2b), which is why the spot matters.

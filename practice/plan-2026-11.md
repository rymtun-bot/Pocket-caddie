## Warm-up (every time, dynamic, no long static holds)
- Patio (5 min): 10 arm circles each way; 10 torso turns with a club across the shoulders; 10 hip hinges with the
  club on your back; 10 leg swings each leg; 5 lunges with a turn each side; 10 hold-off half swings with the PW.
- Range or course (10 min): the patio routine, then 10 swings building from half to full speed, 5 short chips,
  5 putts of 3–6 ft. Long static stretches lowered clubhead speed in small studies; stretch after practice instead.
  Stop if anything hurts.

## Pre-shot routine (every shot, 20–30 seconds)
1. Pocket Caddie: plays-like yardage and club. Aim at the center of the green.
2. Stand behind the ball. Pick the target, then a spot 2–3 ft ahead on that line.
3. One rehearsal swing with the hold-off feel (hands lead through impact).
4. Clubface square to the spot first, then feet, hips and shoulders parallel. Hip-hinge setup.
5. One look at the target, one at the ball, swing within about 5 seconds.
6. After: measure the shot in Pocket Caddie (course) or note start direction (patio).
On the course nothing may be laid down to help alignment (Rules of Golf 10.2b), so the spot replaces the sticks.

## Patio session (30–40 min)
Warm-up · video swings if scheduled (iPad, Slo-mo, one clip per swing, Rapsodo shot order) · club A ×10, club B ×10
(sticks down, Rapsodo 7 ft back on the stick, ball on tape mark, net 8 ft, wait for ready, note missed reads) ·
course mode 5 balls (sticks away, full routine, different club each ball) · speed sets Mon/Wed/Fri (3 × 5 light swings
each side) · send Rapsodo screenshots and video.

## Daily putting (10 min, 6 ft rug + gate trainer)
10 putts from 3 ft, 10 from 5 ft through the gate (log best streak), then 5 with the full routine.

## Home short-game session (30 min)
- Chipping, 15 min: on the patio mat, a towel (about 3 ft square) as the landing spot at 5, 10 and 15 ft carry.
  8- or 9-iron bump-and-run and PW: 10 balls per distance. Count how many land on the towel.
- Up-and-down game, two parts, no walking back and forth. Outside: chip 9 balls at the towel and write down each
  result in order (T = on the towel, N = near miss within about one towel-width, M = farther). Later, inside on the rug
  (as part of daily putting): putt one ball for each result in the same order: T = 3 ft, N = 5 ft, M = 6 ft.
  Each putt holed = an up-and-down. Goal: 5 of 9.
- The Rapsodo is not needed for chips (it struggles to read very short shots).

## Home round (course mode, 40 min)
Play 9 holes of Westchester (Blue) or Rancho (White or Blue) into the net with the full routine.
- Each full shot: the Rapsodo carry is the distance gained; the shot "finds the fairway or green" if it starts within
  3° of the line and carries within 10% of the planned yardage. Otherwise play the next shot as a chip.
- Remaining distance = hole yardage minus carries; pick the next club from the Clubs tab.
- Finish each hole with a chip to the towel and the putt on the rug, as in the up-and-down game. Keep score.

## After 11/13 (to January)
Weekly at home: two sessions on priority 1–2 clubs, one wedge-ladder session, one home short-game session, one home
round (alternate Westchester Blue and Rancho), daily putting. Re-film every two weeks; speed re-test every two weeks.
Update this plan when the driver, 3U and Rancho tees are known.

## What home practice can't cover
Real ball flight and landing (patio carries are estimates), lag putts over 6 ft, chips and pitches from grass, and
bunkers. Optional, the golfer's choice: one visit to a range and practice green in late December, before the first
round, to check carries at lasered flags and get a feel for green speed, lag putting and bunker shots.

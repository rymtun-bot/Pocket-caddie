## What decides it
- The 3U (159) and 5U (150) from 145-170 yd (4U out, golfer 2026-11-19): the 3U is the approach on 1, 6, 9 and the tee club
  on 8 and 12; the 5U takes the ~150 approaches on 5, 10, 13, the second shots on 4 and 17, and the tee shots on 15, 16, 18.
  Their consistency matters more than any other club.
- Lay-ups leave a full wedge (~81 54°, ~104 PW, ~111 9i, ~88 PW arms parallel): no part swings.
- Chipping and one-putts: pars after a missed green need a chip inside about 5 ft. Short-game sessions count.
- Blue tees add 10-40 yd per hole: several approaches move from 3U to out of reach; plan Blue separately if needed.

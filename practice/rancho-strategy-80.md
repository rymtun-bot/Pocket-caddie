# Rancho Park, White tees: a plan for 80 (2026-11-04, updated 2026-11-05)

Assumptions (golfer's): two putts on most greens, three putts on 4 holes (40 putts). Par 71, so 80 = 9 over:
4 over from the three-putts + 5 "bogey-allowed" holes (2, 8, 11, 12, 15). Par is the target everywhere else, which needs
a green in regulation or a chip close enough for one putt. No penalty strokes: every choice below avoids OB, the
ditch on 17, and bunkers in the landing zones.

(patio 11/04), 50° untested (Fri 11/6), 54° 81 (patio 11/04). Wedges are played full (or gripped down 1 in), never
part swings (golfer's preference 2026-11-04), so lay-ups leave a full-wedge distance (~81, ~100, ~110).

(courses/rancho_park.md), about ±15-20 yd. The golfer's driver miss is a right start, so aims lean left of center.

## What decides it
- The 4U and 5U from 140-165 yd: the 4U is the approach club on holes 1, 6, 9, 13 and the tee club on 8, 12, 15, 16, 18;
  the 5U takes the ~150 approaches on 5 and 10 and the 141-yd 3rd. Their consistency matters more than any other club.
- 5U carry is still a PRGR estimate (~147). Confirm it on the Rapsodo (6 shots each 5U and 4U); if it differs by 5+ yd,
  update this plan and CARDS.rancho.plan.
- Lay-ups leave a full wedge (~81 54°, ~100 PW, ~110 9i): no part swings.
- Chipping and one-putts: pars after a missed green need a chip inside about 5 ft. Short-game sessions count.
- Blue tees add 10-40 yd per hole: several approaches move from 4U to out of reach; plan Blue separately if needed.

# What to do in each session, Oct 31 – Dec 1

Every day, also: **10 min putting on the rug** (3 ft ×10, 5 ft ×10 through the gate, then 5 with the full routine)
and **5 min visualization**. Thursdays: putting and visualization only.

## Session types (what to do, step by step)

**M · Measure (35 min)**
1. Warm-up, 5 min (arm circles, torso turns, hip hinges, leg swings, lunges, 10 hold-off half swings with PW).
2. Setup: alignment stick ball → net, Rapsodo 7 ft back on the stick, ball on the tape mark, net 8 ft.
3. Club 1: 10 balls. Wait for the ready signal. Note any missed read.
4. Club 2: 10 balls, same way.
5. Course mode: stick away, 5 balls, a different club each ball, full pre-shot routine.
6. If marked **+S**: speed sets at the end, 3 × 5 light swings each side.
7. Send: Rapsodo shot list screenshot for each club.

**V · Video + measure (45 min)**: a Measure session, but after the warm-up film 5 swings with the iPad
(Slo-mo, one clip per swing, Rapsodo running). Send the clips or the Swing Frames picture with the Rapsodo screenshot.

**P3 · Par-3 game (30 min)**: warm-up, then 1 ball per hole at the listed yardages, club nearest the yardage from the
Clubs tab, full routine. Write each start direction and carry. Then repeat the holes you missed.

**HC · Patio Home Course (40 min for 9 holes, 80 for 18)**: warm-up, then open Pocket Caddie → Setup → Home Course,
pick course, tees and holes, and play with the full routine. Chips to the towel; putts on the rug now or later.
Send "Copy round for Claude".

**SG · Short game (30 min)**: chipping to the towel at 5, 10 and 15 ft carry, 10 balls each (8- or 9-iron
bump-and-run and PW), then the up-and-down game: chip 9 balls and write T / N / M; putt them later on the rug.

**BT · Ball test (30 min)**: warm-up, then for each ball (Supersoft, PXG Xtreme Tour X, Trust Bison Soft):
5 × 7-iron and 5 × driver. Send the Rapsodo lists labelled by ball.

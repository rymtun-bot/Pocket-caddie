# Westchester, Blue tees: a plan for 74 (2026-11-04, updated 2026-11-05)

Same assumptions as the Rancho plan: two putts on most greens, three putts on 4 holes. Par 64, so 74 = 10 over:
4 from the three-putts + 6 "bogey-allowed" holes, chosen from the hardest by handicap (3, 17, 2, 16, 8, 14).
Par is the target on the other 12 holes, with no penalty strokes.

Clubs (updated 2026-11-05 after the bag decision: 5-iron out, 5U back in): Driver 195, Cleveland 3W 180 (tee only),
4U 154, 5U ~147 (PRGR patio 10/28; confirm on the Rapsodo), 6i/7i ~125 (patio 11/04: 124/122), 8i 113, 9i 109, PW 100
(patio 11/04), 50° untested (Fri 11/6), 54° 81 (patio 11/04). Wedges are played full (or gripped down 1 in), never
part swings (golfer's preference 2026-11-04), so lay-ups leave a full-wedge distance (~81, ~100, ~110).
Roll about 10 yd for driver/3W, 5 for the 4U. Hazards: courses/westchester.md (course map scaled to the
scorecard, about ±15-20 yd). Driver miss = right start, so aims lean left of center unless trouble is on the left.

## What decides it
- Par 3s from 141 to 160 yards: holes 4, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 16. The 5U now takes 4, 8 (gripped down), 12, 13 and 14,
  the 4U 11 and 16. The 5U carry is still a PRGR estimate (~147): confirm it on the Rapsodo; if it differs by 5+ yd,
  update this plan and CARDS.westchester.plan.

- Hole 2: the pond is the only water on the course. The 3W keeps the tee shot short of it.

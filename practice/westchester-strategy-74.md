# Westchester, Blue tees: a plan for 74 (2026-11-04)

Same assumptions as the Rancho plan: two putts on most greens, three putts on 4 holes. Par 64, so 74 = 10 over:
4 from the three-putts + 6 "bogey-allowed" holes, chosen from the hardest by handicap (3, 17, 2, 16, 8, 14).
Par is the target on the other 12 holes, with no penalty strokes.

Clubs (Clubs tab, 2026-11-04): Driver 195, Cleveland 3W 180 (tee only), 4U 154, 7i 128, 8i 113, 9i 109, PW 96, 50° 72,
54° 66; roll about 10 yd for driver/3W, 5 for the 4U. Hazards: courses/westchester.md (course map scaled to the
scorecard, about ±15-20 yd). Driver miss = right start, so aims lean left of center unless trouble is on the left.

## What decides it
- Par 3s from 141 to 160 yards: holes 4, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 16. Seven of the ten par 3s sit in the 7i-4U gap
  (128-154). The 4U has to be reliable, and the 5U (~147) would fit 4, 12 and 13 exactly: test 4U vs 5U at the range.
- Short par 4s (5, 6, 7, 18): a good tee shot leaves a wedge. Wedge distances (Wednesday's ladder) set up these pars.
- Hole 2: the pond is the only water on the course. The 3W keeps the tee shot short of it.

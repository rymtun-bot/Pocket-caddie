MY PRE-SHOT ROUTINE

FULL SHOTS (irons, hybrids, 3-wood) - about 20-25 seconds
1. Behind the ball: pick the target, then a spot 2-3 ft
   in front of the ball on that line. Aim at the spot.
2. Two practice swings to the tempo count (1.05/0.35):
   feel the shift and turn, hold the finish.
3. Step in: clubface to the spot first, then feet, hips,
   shoulders parallel to that line.
4. One look at the target, one look at the spot.
5. One cue, then go: "Press left, then turn."

DIFFERENCES BY SHOT

DRIVER
- Pick: a side of the fairway (left-centre; my miss is right)
- Rehearse: 2 swings at about 75% effort
- Setup: grip down 2 in, ball off left heel,
  tee half the ball above the crown
- Cue: "Smooth, press left, then turn"
- Tempo: 1.05/0.35

IRONS / HYBRIDS / 3-WOOD
- Pick: target + spot 2-3 ft ahead
- Rehearse: 2 swings to the tempo count
- Setup: per club checklist (ball position, weight)
- Cue: "Press left, then turn"
- Tempo: 1.05/0.35

PITCH (30-60 yd, hip high / arms parallel)
- Pick: landing spot + how far it will roll
- Rehearse: 2 swings to the chosen length, eyes on landing spot
- Setup: weight 55-60% left, ball middle, stance narrower
- Cue: "Finish as long as the backswing"
- Tempo: 1.05/0.35 (same as full)

CHIP (under 30 yd)
- Pick: landing spot on the green + club for the roll
  (lots of green 9i, some PW, little 54/58)
- Rehearse: 2-3 small swings looking at the landing spot
- Setup: weight 60-70% left, ball back, feet close
- Cue: "Chest turns, hands ahead"
- Tempo: "back ... thru" (0.53/0.27)

PUTT
- Pick: read from behind the ball, then from the low side
- Rehearse: 2 strokes looking at the hole (feel the length)
- Setup: line on ball along the start line, eyes over ball
- Cue: "Rock the shoulders"
- Tempo: 0.60/0.30

AFTER EVERY SHOT
- Hold the finish 2-3 seconds
- Accept the result; if it was a bad one: breathe,
  "It's OK. Be confident." Next shot.
